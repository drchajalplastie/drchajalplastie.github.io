Frequently asked questions

How do I book an appointment?

Call the Plastic Surgery Department for guidance on the appointment process.

Do I need a referral?

The Plastic Surgery Department can tell you whether a referral is needed for your situation.

Where should a referral be sent?

The department can confirm how to send a referral and which documents to include. Its details are listed on the Contact page.

Which documents should I bring?

When confirming your appointment, ask the department which documents or results to bring.

What conditions fall within her practice?

See the Expertise page for documented areas of expertise. The department can advise you based on your situation.
