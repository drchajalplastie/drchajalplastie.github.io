About

Dr. Charlotte Jaloux is a plastic and hand surgeon at the Montreal General Hospital. She joined the plastic surgery team in June 2025.

Training and experience

Marseille
Medical degree from Aix-Marseille University in 2012, followed by residency training in plastic and reconstructive surgery in Marseille.
Paris
Hand surgery fellowship at the Institut de la Main, with a particular focus on surgery for upper-limb spasticity.
Hôpital de la Timone
Former head of hand surgery and limb reconstruction at Hôpital de la Timone in Marseille.

Research

Clinical Associate Professor of Surgery at McGill University. Dr. Jaloux holds a PhD in neuroscience. Her research interests include upper-limb functional recovery and peripheral nerve regeneration.
